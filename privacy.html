SAMUSIGNAL PRO

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 8 August 2026

This policy explains how SamuSignal Pro ("the app") handles your information. The short version: the app has no server of its own, no user accounts, and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We collect nothing. SamuSignal Pro has no backend server, no database, no login, no tracking pixels and no advertising SDKs. The developer cannot see your data, your trades, your API keys or your usage.

2. Data Stored On Your Device

The app saves the following in your device's local browser storage so that it survives between sessions:

  • Your API keys for TwelveData and, if you add one, Google Gemini
  • Your Telegram bot token and chat ID, if you choose to set them up
  • Your trade journal — open positions, closed history and signal records
  • Your settings, watchlist, theme and per-pair TP/SL preferences
This data never leaves your device except when it is sent directly to the third-party services listed below, which you enable yourself. You can erase all of it at any time from Settings → Delete All Data, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Third-Party Services

When you use certain features, your device connects directly to these providers. The app is not an intermediary — no data passes through any server belonging to the developer.

  • TwelveData — market prices and candles. Receives your API key and the symbols you request. (privacy policy)
  • Google Gemini — optional AI explanations. Receives your API key and the numerical signal data for the pair you analyse. Only used when you tap "Ask the AI". (privacy policy)
  • Telegram — optional alerts. Receives your bot token and the alert text you choose to send. (privacy policy)
  • ForexFactory — the public economic calendar feed.
  • TradingView — the embedded price chart widget.

Each of these services applies its own privacy policy to the data it receives. Please review them before enabling a feature.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications (optional) — used only to alert you when a signal arrives or a take-profit or stop-loss level is reached. Notifications are generated on your device; nothing is sent to a push server.
  • Storage — used to keep your journal and settings on the device.
  • Network — used to fetch prices, news and, if enabled, AI replies.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

5. Children

SamuSignal Pro is a financial analysis tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and no information is knowingly collected from them.

6. Data Security

Because nothing is transmitted to us, there is no central store to breach. Your API keys are held in your device's local storage. Treat your device security as your data security: anyone with access to an unlocked device can open the app. Export a backup file regularly, since uninstalling the app or clearing browser data will permanently erase everything.

7. Changes To This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new date at the top.

8. Contact

For any question about this policy or the app, contact the developer through the support channel listed on the app's Google Play listing.

9. Financial Disclaimer

SamuSignal Pro is an analysis and journaling tool. It is not financial advice.

The signals, scores, agent votes, backtests and AI commentary it produces are generated from technical indicators and are for informational and educational purposes only. They are not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Trading foreign exchange, gold, indices and cryptocurrencies on margin carries a high level of risk and can result in the loss of more than your initial deposit. Past performance, including any backtest shown in this app, does not predict future results. The app does not connect to any broker and cannot place, modify or close real orders — every trade you make is your own decision, executed by you with your own broker. You are solely responsible for your trading decisions and any resulting losses. If you are unsure, seek advice from a licensed financial professional.
